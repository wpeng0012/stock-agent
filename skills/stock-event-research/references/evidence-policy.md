# Evidence policy

## Source levels

1. Official exchange or CNInfo announcement: primary evidence.
2. Exchange investor Q&A or structured event aggregation: supporting evidence; verify material facts against announcements.
3. Company website or established financial news: context and independent reporting.

## Required distinctions

- `planned`, `approved`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `terminated`, and `unknown` are different stages.
- Investor Q&A is a public company response, not a statutory filing.
- A news article that repeats an announcement is not independent confirmation.
- Missing sources and failed collection must appear in `data_gaps`.

## Impact wording

Use `positive`, `negative`, `neutral`, or `uncertain` only as a conditional research assessment. Include `short_term`, `medium_term`, or `long_term` and explain uncertainty. Never state that an event guarantees a price move.
